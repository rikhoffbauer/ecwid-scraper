## Design Context

### Users
One technical operator uses Catalogue as a personal product-intelligence workspace to research tracked products, compare offers, inspect changes, manage sources, and collaborate with an AI assistant.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, editorial.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first editorial intelligence: a premium product catalogue combined with an analyst's research desk. Warm stone surfaces, ink text, forest-green actions, restrained rust change signals, serif display typography, compact humanist UI typography, and almost no decorative effects.

### Design Principles
- Keep one contextual sidebar on each side, switched by slim outer rails.
- Keep the catalogue and current task visually dominant.
- Reveal advanced controls progressively instead of displaying every option.
- Make the assistant a working surface with trusted inline widgets.
- Keep external source behavior visibly and permanently read-only.
